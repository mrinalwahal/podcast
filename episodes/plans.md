## Kumar Anand — *confirmed (first guest)*

Economist / policy veteran: **banking, NPAs, public finance, economic history, agriculture** — the closest fit to the monetary spine. Fluent Hindi + English long-form (*Janhit Junction*). Co-author of *Quick & Concise: Economics* (Hachette, 2026) with Akash Kumar.
⚠️ **The 2026 book bio refers to his CCS / NIPFP roles in the past tense** — confirm his current affiliation before booking or making a thumbnail.

**Already planned — Session One (the monetary arc):** #1 *Inflation as a hidden tax* · #2 *Money without the state* → [kumar_anand/one/host.md](kumar_anand/one/host.md).

**Recommended next sessions (ranked):**

1. **Who really pays when a bank goes bad?** *(banking / NPAs — record as his Round Two: tightest bridge from the money spine)*
   PSU-bank recapitalisation / a big loan write-off → **politically-directed credit → bad loans → the saver-taxpayer foots the bill = the Cantillon effect inside banking** (connected borrowers first, savers last). · Steelman priority-sector lending / lender-of-last-resort; concede private banks fail too (Yes Bank, Global Trust). *His NPA / public-sector-bank work is the direct fit.*

2. **How India broke and remade itself** *(economic history / 1991)*
   The 1991 crisis — three weeks of reserves, the gold airlift → **License Raj → the forex-fiscal crash central planning produced → liberalisation → mass poverty reduction, honestly uneven** (flat real wages; "crony socialism → stigmatised capitalism"). *Indian economic history is his stated core; this is the show's existing "1991 story," and he's the guest for it.*

3. **The most expensive vote India buys** *(farm loan waivers / fiscal populism)*
   An election-season waiver → **vote-buying → who pays (fiscal → borrowing / inflation → the hidden tax again) → moral hazard + seen-vs-unseen.** · Concede agri markets are genuinely rigged (APMC / MSP) — cronyism cuts both ways. *He did the* Seen & Unseen *farm-loan-waivers episode.*

4. **Reading the business news without getting fooled** *(data literacy — lighter, clippable)*
   A scary GDP / CPI headline → **how official statistics are built and gamed → why no single print is trustworthy → the perishable-stats honesty principle.**

## Akash Kumar — *new; recommend booking*

Kumar's co-author. Founder/CEO of **Raaz**; IIT Madras; ex-**Students For Liberty** (South Asia); **Mises** contributor; Ayn Rand origin story — the **Austrian-leaning "radical substance" pole.** Already an experienced **podcast host / panel-moderator** (has interviewed Dave Rubin, Mark Lutter, Nitin Pai) — the most camera-ready guest on the list. Handles: **@aye_kaash**, akashkumar.co.

**Recommended episodes (ranked; open with #1):**

1. **The resource curse: the richest ground under the poorest people** *(development / institutions — best opener for him)*
   Congo cobalt / Venezuela oil → then bring it home: *why is resource-rich Jharkhand poor?* → **rentier states don't need to tax citizens → no accountability → prosperity comes from institutions and sound money, not stuff** (petro-states & the printing press bridge straight to the money spine). · Steelman geography / Dutch disease; concede markets alone didn't fix Congo.

2. **The case for shutting down BSNL** *(deregulation / creative destruction)*
   A zombie PSU kept alive — *he wrote this exact op-ed* → **why the state props up incumbents → the unseen cost: the competitor never born → pro-market, not pro-business.** · Concede privatisation can breed *private* monopolies (the crony risk).

3. **Every trade makes both sides richer** *(the moral case for markets)*
   A street-market haggle / a chai transaction → **voluntary exchange is positive-sum vs. the zero-sum instinct → protectionism as a tax on your own people → why "pro-market" isn't "pro-rich."** · Concede not all exchange is truly voluntary (coercion, monopsony) — mark the honest boundary. *The Rand / Mises moral case is his origin story.*

4. **Zero to one in a License Raj** *(entrepreneurship / the permission-to-work state)*
   His own founder story (Raaz) vs. the compliance maze → **the entrepreneur as value-creator → the businesses never started, the jobs never created.** Lived experience; strong clips.

5. **Superstar economics: why Kohli earns 4× Rohit** *(inequality vs. fairness)*
   The wage gap → **winner-take-all markets & marginal productivity → separate *earned* success from *rigged* success → bridge to Kumar: asset inflation and the Cantillon effect widen inequality.**

**Structure notes:** keep him a **separate guest**, not a joint panel with Kumar (format = host + one guest; separate sessions = 2× episodes + 2× cross-promo). If you ever want a second on-air voice, **he's the co-host to experiment with** — don't change the format on spec, but he's the candidate.
