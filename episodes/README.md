## Season 1 — the monetary spine (10 episodes)

Plain, descriptive titles. Each opens on a specific story and drives into the theory. Front-load the relatable; earn the radical conclusions as the season builds.

| # | Topic | Opening story / on-ramp | The theory it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **What is money** | POW-camp cigarettes; Yap stone money | Money emerges from markets (Menger); inflation is monetary; Cantillon |
| 2 | **Central banks & the money monopoly** | The RBI everyone's heard of | The money monopoly; the case against a central bank (Hayek) |
| 3 | **The Cantillon effect** | Who gets the new money first | Inflation as a hidden, regressive tax + cronyism engine |
| 4 | **Demonetisation** | 8 Nov 2016 | State power over money; money & consent |
| 5 | **Central bank digital currency** | UPI you use daily → the e₹ | CBDC: programmable, surveillable money vs. a free society |
| 6 | **Gold & sound money** | Every household's gold | Money the state can't print |
| 7 | **The Austrian business cycle** | Loan melas, NPAs, 2008 | Cheap money, booms and busts; interest rates as signals |
| 8 | **Bitcoin & hard money** | India's crypto tax/ban fights | Hard money; honestly steelman *and* attack it |
| 9 | **Free banking** | "But who else could run it?" | Money without a central bank; competing currencies |
| 10 | **Taxation** | Your payslip's deductions | The ancap case — earned, to a statist India |

**Notes:** Eps 1–3 build the core (what money is → who controls it → who it robs). Eps 4–6 are the widely-felt middle (demonetisation, CBDC, gold — strong Indian resonance, good clips). Eps 7–9 go deeper; Ep 10 turns outward to the broader ancap case. Keep 1–2 episodes banked; let clip data reorder the rest.

## Topic bank (future seasons — broader ancap, routed through money & markets)

**Indian monetary history & the state:** the 1991 crisis & the gold airlift · financial repression (how the state funds itself off your savings) · capital controls (why you can't freely move your own money out) · the rupee's century of debasement · hyperinflation stories (Weimar, Zimbabwe, Venezuela) and what they teach.
**Money debates:** MMT vs. sound money (the great debate) · UPI — did India build great money rails, and who really controls them? · **the insurance rip-off** (mis-selling & incentives) — *guest: Monika Halan.*
**The wider ancap case (money as the wedge):** Is taxation theft? · Can law be private? (polycentric law, honestly) · Who will build the roads? (public goods, taken seriously) · Why a bigger state breeds *more* cronyism · Price controls & shortages (onions, rent, fuel — the seen & unseen) · The License Raj, then and now · The entrepreneur as hero — the moral case for profit.

## Booking tactics
- **Warm intros beat cold emails.** Kotasthane bridges Takshashila/Puliyabaazi; Rajagopalan bridges Mercatus/EV India; Ajay Shah bridges XKDR/FED.
- **Lead with the audience, not the ask** — "a tight, honest, sound-money show that actually steelmans the other side" is a proposition serious people *want*.
- **Make it frictionless** — Riverside for remote, send the clip + share copy, keep to ~45–60 min. **Ask every guest for two more names.**

## Recurring formats
- **"Explain it to me" shorts** (one monetary idea, 8–12 min) — feeds the clips engine.
- **"The number that surprised me."**
- **Steelman/debate specials** — a genuine statist or MMT guest; best tape, best credibility.
