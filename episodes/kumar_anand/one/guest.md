# For Kumar — Round One: *Inflation as a hidden tax*
*Share with the guest. Host's own sheet: [host.md](host.md).*

**Gist:** our experimental first episode — relaxed, low-stakes, learning as we go. Topic: **inflation as a *political* thing**, not CPI prints.

**Through-line:** money loses value on purpose → whoever prints it spends it first, you get it last → **a tax nobody voted for.** *Your public-finance angle (how the state funds itself) is the heart of it.*

**How I run it:**
- No intros — we start mid-conversation, on a story.
- I'll stop you for the plain-Hinglish version of any jargon (a feature, not doubt).
- I'll steelman the RBI / the case for some inflation — help me make it strong, then we push back.
- We concede our own side too (deflation, rigidity). Honesty is the brand.

**Come with (if you get time):**
- An opening **story** on debasement — Weimar / Keynes-on-Lenin / Roman coins / your own.
- Concrete Indian examples of **who gets new money first.**
- One **number** that surprised you; one thing you **changed your mind** about.
- A **book or two** to recommend (I ask at the end).

**Not this round:** the CPI basket / latest print; and we save the money monopoly, demonetisation, the digital rupee and gold/Bitcoin for **Round Two** (same-day record).

**I close by asking:** a book rec + *"what's one question you hoped I'd ask, but didn't?"*

**Logistics:** ~45–60 min, Hinglish, video (in-person or Riverside). We can wander — no pressure.

**Optional skim:** Rothbard, *What Has Government Done to Our Money?* · Hazlitt, *What You Should Know About Inflation* · Vivek Kaul, *Easy Money*.
