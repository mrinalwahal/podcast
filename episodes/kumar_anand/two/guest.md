# For Kumar — Round Two: *Money without the state*
*Share with the guest. Host's own sheet: [host.md](host.md).*

**Gist:** second episode, recorded right after Round One. If Round One was how state money *robs* you (the hidden tax), this is how it *controls* you — and the alternative.

**Through-line:** the money monopoly is control (cancel your cash, or a programmable/surveilled digital rupee) → so *why should the state control money at all?* → what would money beyond the state (gold, competing currencies, Bitcoin) look like — and can it work? *Your economic-historian's stories (the rupee, demonetisation, the RBI) are what I want.*

**How I run it (same as Round One):**
- No intros — we start mid-conversation, on a story.
- I'll stop you for the plain-Hinglish version of any jargon.
- I'll steelman the state *and* the digital rupee — help me make it strong, then we push back.
- We concede our own side (gold was seized in 1933; Bitcoin's volatile; the poor rely on cash + welfare).
- **No investment/crypto tips** — money-as-freedom, not trades.

**Come with (if you get time):**
- A **control story** to open on — demonetisation night, the 1933 US gold seizure, or programmable CBDC money. Your demonetisation memories = gold.
- Your **real view**: gold vs. competing currencies vs. Bitcoin, *for India* — what breaks first.
- One surprising **number**; one thing you **changed your mind** about; a **book** to recommend.

**I close by asking:** a book rec + *"what's one question you hoped I'd ask, but didn't?"*

**Logistics:** ~45–60 min, Hinglish, video, right after Round One.

**Optional skim:** Hayek, *The Denationalisation of Money* · Ammous, *The Bitcoin Standard* · the RBI e₹ concept note (read critically).
